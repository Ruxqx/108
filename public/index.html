Blackjack Play Guide

Try to get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

Card values

  • Number cards count as their number.
  • J, Q, and K count as 10.
  • Aces count as 11, or 1 if 11 would bust your hand.

Your turn

  • Choose Hit to take another card.
  • Choose Stand to keep your total and let the dealer play.
  • If your total goes over 21, you bust and lose the bet.

Dealer rules

  • The dealer reveals their hidden card after you stand.
  • The dealer keeps drawing until their hand is at least 17.
  • If the dealer busts, you win.

Results and chips

  • A win pays your bet amount.
  • A blackjack pays one and a half times your bet.
  • A push is a tie, so no chips are won or lost.
  • If a logged-in player hits 0 chips, all logged-in players get 200 chips.
